# Intake — Logo renders microscopically

**Date:** 2026-09-18
**Status:** implemented — on staging, verified visually
**Process:** v9.51 (see `PROCESS_AGENTS.md`)
**Parent intake:** `2026-09-17-p4-logo-visibility.md` — chip landed,
legible but too small

## Problem statement

The logo on the sign-in page is a roughly 20px tile — the mark inside
is a thin-line illustration and at that size it reads as a speck. On
the auth shell the corner mark and the mark above "Welcome back" are
both too small to identify. The chip fix solved contrast, not scale.

## Current state

- `Logo` renders `size-5`/`size-6` (20–24px) at every call site —
  sized for the old compact Comp AI glyph, not a detailed lockup mark.
- The mynaani mark is a fine-line portrait illustration; it needs
  ~2–3× more pixels to read.
- The full `mynaani-logo.webp` lockup (icon + wordmark + tagline)
  exists but is unused in the UI.

## Required state

1. The logo is visibly identifiable on sign-in — no squinting.
2. Sizing respects `docs/design.md` (restraint, no giant hero logo),
   but is sized for *this* artwork, not the old glyph.
3. Both variants used correctly: mark for small spots, full lockup
   where there is room (auth shell panel, form header).

## Edge cases

- Scaling the tile to ~40px in the form header reads well; the same
  in the tiny corner chip may be oversized — different call sites may
  need different sizes, which is already supported via className.
- At larger sizes the white chip border/shadow becomes more visible —
  verify it still looks like a deliberate brand tile, not a box.
- `company-mark`/`company-sheet` call sites in the landing mock are
  mock-company avatars — keep them as-is unless they look broken.
- Favicon already uses the mark — untouched.

## Open items

- Exact sizes per surface (auth corner, form header, app header).
- Whether the form header uses the full lockup instead of the mark.
