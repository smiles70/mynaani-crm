## Decision matrix

| Approach | Fit | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raise call-site sizes: corner `size-9`, heading `size-10`–`size-12`; mark asset | Zero new machinery; matches design tokens; mark reads at 40px | High |
| Full lockup in the form header | Wordmark text too small at reasonable heights; duplicates page title | Medium |
| SVG trace of the mark | Sharper scaling but a re-drawn asset — out of scope for a size fix | Low |

## Selected approach

Bump the two auth-shell call sites: brand corner to `size-9`,
`AuthHeading` logo to `size-11`. Keep `size-5` inside the app chrome.
Verify visually on staging before production.
